How totals work

Pickup adds round-trip distance × your cost per kilometre to the item price, plus any fixed costs such as parking or transit fare. For transit, set the kilometre rate to zero and enter the total fare as a fixed cost. Online delivery adds shipping and any fees you enter. Enter tax in fees if you want it included. Amounts are in CAD.

The optional time ranking adds the value of time you spend travelling. Delivery ETAs are shown separately. Unavailable items and offers with unknown stock or missing costs remain visible, but are excluded from the ranked picks.
